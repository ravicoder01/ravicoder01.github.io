Research · ongoing

Grounded legal Q&A in Hindi and Assamese

Can open-source LLMs write correct question–answer pairs from Indian court judgments in low-resource languages? I built a benchmark pipeline that generates Q&A from each section of a judgment, keeps only answers that are actually in the source text, and scores them.

  1. SegmentSplit each judgment into its 5 parts and chunk them into ~700-character pieces.
  2. PromptInstructions written in the target language, asking for JSON Q&A pairs.
  3. GenerateRun each model in 4-bit on a single 16–24 GB GPU, with resumable runs.
  4. GroundDrop duplicates and any answer not found in the source chunk.
  5. ScoreBLEU, ROUGE-1/2/L and BERTScore (XLM-RoBERTa-large) per segment.

LegalEase

A legal assistant for three Indian acts (POCSO, RTI, Consumer Protection). It routes each question to the right act, retrieves the relevant sections and answers only from them. Off-topic questions get a polite "I don't know".
